# Access audit for the Ne I and Cl V final journal articles

The objective was to acquire the original final journal PDFs for two electron-excitation sources through public publisher or legitimate institutional repository routes. Neither PDF was obtained. No access restriction was bypassed, no author was contacted, and no preprint or indexed text was treated as the original final article. The machine-readable route log is in `validation_2026-10-10/source_access_final_articles/access_log.json`.

The routes already tested in the [Ne I source audit](source_validity_ne_i_mg_i.md) and the [Cl V source audit](validation_2026-10-10/clv_ground_excitation/README.md) were not retried. In particular, the previously attempted APS PDF, A&AS `ds1586.pdf`, public ADS scan, CiteSeerX PDF, and ResearchGate PDF routes supplied no original file. Search indexing of journal-formatted text cannot establish that its text or tables match the publisher's original PDF character for character.

The unresolved Ne I claims are the detailed near-threshold calculation and energy-grid treatment in the final article, whether it publishes or bounds Maxwellian effective strengths, and exactly how the 2024 CHIANTI preparation maps its calculated cross sections to the 1,000–1,000,000 K tabulation. Its ADS abstract supports the much narrower statement that the calculation addresses incident-electron energies from threshold to 300 eV, with 457 target states in jK coupling. A maximum electron impact energy is not a verified Maxwellian-temperature validity ceiling.

The unresolved Cl V claims are the exact wording and values of final Table 5, the 21-state close-coupling method and resonance treatment as printed, the paper's justification of its temperature interval, and whether the printed 40,000 K strength lies beyond the methods' stated 30,000 K support. These points are provisionally inferred from indexed journal-formatted text and remain unverified against the original final PDF. Neither the provisional 1,000–40,000 K table nor MOCASSIN's 50,000–200,000 K continuation can establish a published 100,000 K source range.

To resolve those claims, obtain the publishers' original final PDFs for the citations above through authorized library access or from an archive that demonstrably holds the unchanged Version of Record. Read the Methods and tables with `pdftotext -layout`, inspect the rendered pages, then reconcile the source audits. No production source, atom, or cooling file was changed during this access audit.
